# PayYigi platform facts (the AI treats this as the only source of truth)

## What the app offers
- PayYigi lets users sell crypto for Naira (NGN).
- The left menu has: Dashboard, Sell Crypto, Transactions, Settings. Logout is at the bottom left.
- Buying crypto, swapping crypto and a separate withdraw screen are NOT in the app menu. Do not tell users they can do these.

## Requirements to sell
- Email verified, NIN verified (shown in Settings as KYC Status > Identity Verification: COMPLETED), a transaction PIN set, a saved bank account, and an active (not suspended) account.
- The user's transaction PIN is required to confirm a sell.
- There are NO account tiers.

## How to sell crypto (step by step)
1. Click "Sell Crypto" in the left menu (or the "Sell Crypto" button at the top right of the Dashboard).
2. Under "Select Asset", open the dropdown and pick the coin. Each option shows the coin name, its network, and its minimum amount.
3. Type the amount in the box labelled "Amount Of (COIN) In USD". The amount is entered in USD.
4. The "Payout Summary" on the right shows "You will receive", Rate, Amount and Network.
5. Click "Continue" and follow the on-screen steps.
- The Naira amount shown is an estimate. The final amount depends on the live market rate at the time the crypto is confirmed, so it can vary slightly.
- There is no separate withdrawal feature. When a user sells crypto, the payout goes directly to the bank account they set as default in Settings. Banks differ, but it usually reflects within a few minutes. Do not state longer times than this. If it hasn't arrived, tell the user to check the status on the Transactions page and offer to pass it to the support team.

## Dashboard
- Five cards: Wallet Balance (in NGN), Total Sold, Total Transactions, Locked Balance, Pending.
- "Trending Market" shows live Naira prices for BTC, ETH and USDT.
- "Recent Activities" shows the latest transactions, with a "View all" link.

## Transactions page
- Titled "Transaction History". Filters: All Status, All Assets, and a date picker.
- Columns: Order ID, Asset, Naira Value, Amount, Date, Status, Actions (three-dot menu).
- Statuses: pending, processing, awaiting_crypto (waiting for the user to send the crypto), confirming (waiting for blockchain confirmations), converting, completed, failed, cancelled, refunded, expired.

## Settings page (Profile Settings)
- Bank Accounts: "+ Add" to add an account, a trash icon to delete one, and "Set as default" to choose the account payouts go to.
- Profile: Full Name, Email (with a VERIFIED badge), Phone Number, Date of Birth, and a "Save Changes" button. Only name and date of birth can be updated here.
- KYC Status shows Identity Verification as COMPLETED once verified.
- Two-Factor Authentication has an on/off toggle.
- Account Settings has: Notifications (on/off toggle), Transaction Pin (create or change), and Help & Support ("Send us a message"), which is where users reach support.

## Limits
- Daily withdrawal limit: NGN 3,000,000 for every NIN-verified user.

## Security
- PayYigi staff never ask for passwords, PINs, OTPs or 2FA codes.
